About Parmana

Parmana is infrastructure that protects institutional authority. As AI agents, applications, and automated systems increasingly initiate real-world actions inside businesses, from payments to records to access, the question every institution now faces is the same: can something act without permission it was never given?

Parmana answers that question structurally. Every proposed action is checked against what an institution has explicitly authorized, before it executes. Nothing acting on a business's behalf, whether a person, software, an AI agent, or an attacker, holds the credentials to act on its own. An action either matches what was authorized and proceeds, or it doesn't and is refused, with a verifiable record either way.

The company is not commercialized yet. Parmana is currently working with design partners and undergoing security testing ahead of wider release.
